## Product (source: https://atomrahome.com and https://atomrahome.com/pages/f-a-q)
- ATOMRA HOME candle sand wax is granulated wax ("candle sand") used to make a candle in a non-flammable container.
- Available as white, colored and full-color black candle sand.
- The black candle sand is fully reprocessed (melted, recolored and regranulated) so the color stays locked inside; it will not stain hands, vases or surfaces and has no artificial dye smell.
- All Atomra candle sand wax is unscented; customers can add their own candle fragrance.
- Pack formats shown on the shop: 500 g with 10 wicks; "Granule Box" 750 g with 20 wicks; "Granule box" 1.5 kg with 20 wicks; 3 kg with 50 wicks.
- One wick lasts about 15 hours.
- How it works: pour the sand wax into a non-flammable container and flatten it; insert the wick leaving at most 1 cm (1/4 inch) out of the wax; light it. After burning, extinguish and let the wax harden; relight the same wick or replace it to refresh the candle.
- Container guidance: the container must not catch fire, be at least 8 cm (3 inches) wide at its narrowest point, keep at least 5 cm (2 inches) of sand wax around each wick, use only one wick in an 8 cm wide container, and leave at least 1 inch between the bottom of the wick and the base.
