Frequently asked questions

How does the candle sand work?
Pour the sand wax into a non-flammable container and flatten it; insert the wick leaving at most 1 cm (1/4 inch) out of the wax; light it. After burning, extinguish and let the wax harden; relight the same wick or replace it to refresh the candle.
Which container should I use?
The container must not catch fire, be at least 8 cm (3 inches) wide at its narrowest point, keep at least 5 cm (2 inches) of sand wax around each wick, use only one wick in an 8 cm wide container, and leave at least 1 inch between the bottom of the wick and the base.
